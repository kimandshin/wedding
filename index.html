Our Vows

Today, before God, our families, and friends, we promise to walk together in faith and love. We will encourage one another, forgive quickly, and choose grace over pride.

In seasons of joy and in seasons of weakness, we will seek God first, listen deeply, and protect this covenant with patience and honesty. With God’s help, we will build a home filled with warmth, prayer, and laughter.
